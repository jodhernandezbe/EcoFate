# UI audit, 2026-11-09

Third run of `/ui-audit` on the EcoFate frontend (scope: all surfaces), the first since accounts
were added (`features/auth/`, the session gate in `App.tsx`, the rail's Account entry and
admin-only Monitoring, the Account column in `SessionsTable`). Reference: `frontend/DESIGN.md`;
heuristics H1 to H10 (`ui-craft` skill, `references/audit-heuristics.md`). The audit changed no
application code.

## Scope and method

- **Approved references** (`frontend/src/**/__screenshots__/`, 32 PNGs, light and dark): all
  looked at. Auth (sign in, sign-in error, sign up, check email, forgot), chat interruptions
  (resumable, bad key, busy, final), exposure chain (both lanes wide and narrow, community only,
  worker lane only, worker endpoint detail), overview ribbon (empty, ready). Values are the repo's
  synthetic fixtures.
- **Temporary visual test** (Vitest browser mode, Playwright Chromium, 1320 × 900, light and
  dark): a throwaway `src/AuditTemp.visual.tsx` rendered the whole `App` with `fetch` stubbed by
  the existing helpers (`test/session.tsx` `ADMIN`/`MEMBER`, the `SESSION` row of
  `SessionsTable.test.tsx`, `test/fixtures.ts`, `test/graphs.ts`) and the live server's public
  `GET /api/v1/settings/client` answer (layout, thresholds, suggestions), so the layout is the
  real one. The test file and its output folder were deleted afterwards; 38 screenshots were kept
  in the session scratch directory as `<surface>-<state>-<scheme>.png`.
- **States reached** (light and dark each): session loading, session error; workspace first load
  as admin and as a member without a key; rail collapsed; narrow stack (720 px); overview
  Risk-by-group empty; Exposure chain empty; Account drawer; delete-account dialog, empty and
  with its "Enter your password" error; Monitoring with a completed and a failed session (and,
  in a first pass without the layout config, with a 420 px pane); Settings drawer over
  Monitoring; verify-email pending and failed; reset password, and submitted empty; exposure
  chain re-rendered from current code at 1280 and 600 px.
- **Live app** (`127.0.0.1:8000`): only the public `settings/client` GET was read. No sign-up,
  reset or resend was sent; signed-in states come from fixtures.
- **Static checks** over `frontend/src` (tests, visual files, fixtures and generated types
  excluded): literal colours, shadows, easings and fonts; arbitrary text sizes; radius ladder;
  uppercase outside titles; `?? 0` / dash / `N/A` fallbacks; browser dialogs; hard-coded digits;
  icon-only controls and their names.
- **Not reached**: see "Not assessed".

Scores: 2 good, 1 partial, 0 poor, `–` not testable in this run.

## Ranked proposals

1. **Copy breaks the document's count and absent-value rules** (Risk by group, App first load,
   Chat interrupted, Exposure chain detail, Account screens; H4 H7).
   - Observation: the Risk-by-group view prints "0 facilities in this run, 0 with a worst-case
     result." before any run (`overview-risk-by-group-empty-*`; `OverviewPanel.tsx:107`), against
     "counts are shown only when there are items to count". Its pathway picker reads "Outdoor Air
     → Inhalation → General Population" in Title Case (same screenshot). The tool group header
     says "1 steps" (`stop-*` references; `ToolExecutionPill.tsx:33`). Risk cards close with "Not
     shown: not yet computed." (`RiskMetricCard.tsx:57`, `WorkerRiskCard.tsx:48`), which repeats
     the pill. The chain detail writes "not reported (not reported by the tool)"
     (`worker-endpoint-detail-light`; `DetailPanel.tsx:38,46` print both the phrase and the
     reason). Verify-failed says "Ask for a new one." then "Sign in with your password to get a
     new link." (`verify-failed-light`).
   - Proposal: replace the zero count with one sentence ("No facilities assessed yet.") until a
     facility exists; sentence-case the pathway labels; pluralise "step"; drop the "Not shown"
     line when the reason is "not yet computed"; show the API's reason instead of "not reported"
     when one is given; make the verify error one instruction.
   - Design document impact: Copy: "an absent value shows the API's reason in place of 'not
     reported', never both"; picker labels are sentence case.
   - Effort: S (string changes plus their tests). Value: high (first visit to two views, every chat turn).

2. **The drawer primitive speaks about settings and opens on its least useful control**
   (Account drawer, Settings drawer; H6 H7 H2).
   - Observation: `BottomDrawer` hard-codes "Maximize settings", "Close settings" and "Resize
     settings" (`components/ui/BottomDrawer.tsx:97,105,117`), so the Account drawer announces the
     wrong surface. Both drawers open with the focus ring on the maximize button
     (`account-drawer-*`, `settings-drawer-dark`). In the Account drawer the password fields and
     its sections stretch to about 1,050 px (`account-drawer-light`).
   - Proposal: derive the three names from the drawer's `title` ("Close account"); send initial
     focus to the first field or to Close, not to Maximize; cap the Account drawer's form at a
     readable width (for example `max-w-md`) inside the full-width sheet.
   - Design document impact: Primitives, `BottomDrawer`: "control names include the title; first
     focus goes to the content".
   - Effort: S. Value: high (every drawer, assistive technology users).

3. **The exposure chain is hard to read at its fitted size, and its references are stale**
   (Exposure chain; H2 H3 H5 H9 H7).
   - Observation: re-rendered from current code, the fit leaves wide empty bands above and below
     and scales nodes so their 11 to 13 px text renders near 8 to 9 px
     (`exposure-chain-current-1280-light`); `worker-lane-only-light` shows one lane in a strip
     across a mostly empty canvas. At 600 px the lanes run off the right edge and the minimap
     draws garbled red blocks clipped at the corner, with status by colour only
     (`exposure-chain-current-600-dark`, `ChainCanvas.tsx:153-160`). Node text uses
     `text-[12px]`/`text-[13px]` and `shadow-sm` (`ChainNode.tsx:42,54,58`). The approved
     references (30 Oct) still show the old uppercase badges and pass within 1 %.
   - Proposal: fit with a minimum zoom that keeps node text at the 11 px step and let the user
     pan; give the minimap a fixed frame inside the canvas or drop it on narrow panels; move node
     text to documented steps; regenerate the five chain references after looking at each.
   - Design document impact: Type table: a row for the chain node (title, value) or use existing
     steps; verify-loop note: regenerate references after changing small text.
   - Effort: M. Value: high (the document's signature surface).

4. **Monitoring loses its question column on a narrow pane and hides the new Account column**
   (Monitoring; H9 H10 H2).
   - Observation: three always-shown columns have fixed widths totalling 424 px (`w-48`, `w-28`,
     `w-30`, `SessionsTable.tsx:16-27`); in a 420 px pane the Question column took no width and
     its header printed over "Estimated cost" (first pass, rail at its unconfigured default; that image was overwritten by the second pass, so the claim rests on what was seen and on the widths in code). With
     the configured layout the pane is about 630 px and fine (`monitoring-sessions-*`), but a chat
     at its `max_share` 0.55 on a 1320 px window leaves about 350 px. Account appears only from a
     768 px container (`@3xl`), so administrators see it only in fullscreen.
   - Proposal: shrink Started to a short date below `@lg`, give Question a minimum width, and
     show Account before Model at the medium tier (or fold it under the question as a caption).
   - Design document impact: none.
   - Effort: S. Value: medium (administrators only).

5. **Off-ladder steps keep reappearing** (Shell, Overview, Chat, Account, Map; H3).
   - Observation, from the static checks: `shadow-sm` on eight selected segments, nodes and edge
     labels; `text-[15px]` on drawer sub-headings and inputs although the document reserves it for
     chat; inputs `rounded-xl` against the `rounded-lg` rule; uppercase "Analysis area" in the
     rail (`LeftNavRail.tsx:158`) and the 10 px uppercase brand label on the rail and every auth
     card (`BrandLogo.tsx:148`); hex fallbacks in `layers.ts:32-37`.
   - Proposal: decide once and document: a raised-segment rule (token or `--shadow` variant) for
     selected segments; a "control and sub-section" row at 15 px if it stays; inputs on
     `rounded-lg` or move inputs to `rounded-xl` in the ladder; rail heading in sentence case
     (or list it with panel titles); read fallbacks from one place.
   - Design document impact: Type table row, Radius ladder line, Elevation line, and
     `BrandLogo`'s label listed as an allowed uppercase exception if kept.
   - Effort: M (mechanical, about 15 files). Value: medium.

6. **The map legend dominates an empty map** (Map, App first load, Narrow; H1 H2).
   - Observation: with no facilities, the legend shows four status rows and three sentences in a
     card about 250 × 200 px over a blank canvas (`app-first-load-admin-light`, `app-narrow-light`);
     the basemap fallback note sits at the top as a second message. (The basemap did not load in
     the test browser, which makes the canvas blanker than in use.)
   - Proposal: until there are facilities, collapse the legend to its one sentence ("No facilities
     mapped yet") with the status rows behind a disclosure; keep the full legend once pins exist.
   - Design document impact: none (follows "empty sections take their content height").
   - Effort: S. Value: medium (every first load).

7. **Account screens fall back to browser chrome in two places** (Account screens, Session gate;
   H3 H4 H7).
   - Observation: submitting the reset form empty shows Chromium's "Please fill out this field."
     bubble over the hint and button (`reset-submitted-empty-light`), not the in-card error box
     used elsewhere (`sign-in-error-dark`). The session-check error is a bare red line and a
     button on an empty page, without the hairline box the Error state requires and without the
     brand (`session-error-light`; `App.tsx` gate). "The session could not be checked" is
     system language.
   - Proposal: `noValidate` on the auth forms and show the same `role="alert"` box for empty
     fields; render the gate's loading and error inside `AuthCard` with the error box and
     "We could not reach EcoFate. Check your connection and retry."
   - Design document impact: none.
   - Effort: S. Value: medium (every user, before the workspace).

8. **Failed sessions still give their reason only on hover** (Monitoring; H4).
   - Observation: the "Failed" pill carries `FAILED_HINT` as a `title` (`SessionsTable.tsx:97`);
     nothing visible says why or where to look (`monitoring-sessions-light`).
   - Proposal: a `text-xs text-fg2` line under the pill or in the question cell: "Open to see
     the failed step".
   - Design document impact: none.
   - Effort: S. Value: low to medium.

9. **Welcome block competes with the suggestions, and CAS numbers wrap at their hyphen** (Chat
   welcome; H1 H9).
   - Observation: the brand block, the paragraph and four full-width suggestion cards fill the
     chat column with equal weight; "107-83-" / "5" wraps across lines in the second suggestion
     (`app-first-load-admin-light`).
   - Proposal: drop the brand line from the welcome (it is in the rail), and keep CAS numbers
     unbroken (wrap them in a `whitespace-nowrap` span when rendering suggestions).
   - Design document impact: Copy: "identifiers (CAS numbers) never break across lines".
   - Effort: S. Value: low.

## Design document changes (consolidated)

- Copy: an absent value shows the API's reason in place of "not reported", never both; picker
  and option labels are sentence case; identifiers such as CAS numbers never break.
- Primitives, `BottomDrawer`: control names include the drawer's title; initial focus goes to the
  content (first field) or Close.
- Type and spacing: a row for the chain node's title and value (or a rule that it uses existing
  steps); a "control and sub-section" row at `text-[15px]` if drawer headings and inputs keep it.
- Radius ladder: say where inputs sit (`rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl`) and make the code match.
- Elevation: how a selected segment is raised (a documented shadow step or none), replacing ad-hoc
  `shadow-sm`.
- Anti-patterns or exceptions: list `BrandLogo`'s uppercase label as an allowed exception, or
  remove its uppercase; same for the rail's "Analysis area" heading.
- Verification: after changing small text, regenerate that surface's references (the 1 % tolerance
  hides it); the verify loop already says this, the chain references show it was skipped.
